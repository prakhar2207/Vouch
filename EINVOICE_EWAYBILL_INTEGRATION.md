# SriLekh — e-Invoice & E-Way Bill Integration Architecture

## 1. Statutory Context & Legal Requirements

### e-Invoicing (Rule 48(4) CGST Rules)
- Mandatory for taxpayers with aggregate turnover exceeding **₹5 Crore** in any preceding financial year.
- Applies strictly to B2B supplies, Exports, SEZ supplies, and Credit/Debit Notes.
- Invoices are invalid without an **Invoice Reference Number (IRN)** and **Signed QR Code** generated by the Invoice Registration Portal (IRP).
- **Statutory Cancellation Window**: An IRN can ONLY be cancelled within **24 hours** of generation. After 24 hours, the IRN is permanent on the GST portal, and the transaction can only be reversed by issuing a Credit Note.

### E-Way Bill (Rule 138 CGST Rules)
- Mandatory for consignment movement of goods where consignment value exceeds **₹50,000** (interstate or intrastate as per state rules).
- Consists of **Part-A** (Consignor, Consignee, HSN, Taxable Value, Tax) and **Part-B** (Transporter ID, Vehicle Number, Mode of Transport).
- If vehicle breaks down or transshipment occurs, Part-B must be updated before further movement.

### Idempotency & Replay Protection
- Before dispatching a request to Sandbox, `EInvoiceService.generate_irn()` checks `EInvoiceRecord.objects.filter(voucher=voucher, status='GENERATED')`.
- If an active IRN already exists, the service returns the existing record immediately (`is_cached=True`), preventing accidental duplicate IRN generation.

### Statutory 24-Hour Cancellation Workflow
- `EInvoiceService.cancel_irn(irn, reason, remarks)` checks `ack_date`.
- If `current_time - ack_date > 24 hours`, the service rejects cancellation with:
  *"Statutory limit exceeded: IRN cannot be cancelled after 24 hours. Issue a Credit Note instead."*
- If within 24 hours, dispatches `POST /gst/compliance/e-invoice/tax-payer/invoice/cancel` to Sandbox and marks the record `status='CANCELLED'`.

## 3. E-Way Bill Architecture (Part-A & Part-B)

`EWayBillService` provides three statutory workflows:

### 1. E-Way Bill Generation from Sales Voucher
- **Part-A Data**: Document number, invoice date, consignor and consignee details, HSN codes, and taxable values.
- **Part-B Data**: Mode of transport (`1` Road, `2` Rail, `3` Air, `4` Ship), vehicle number, transporter ID/name, and distance in km.
- **Persisted Data**: EWB number, creation date, validity period (`valid_until` calculated based on 200 km/day for normal cargo or 20 km/day for over-dimensional cargo).

### 2. Vehicle Update (Part-B)
- Mandatory when the transporting vehicle breaks down, is changed at a transshipment hub, or consignment is transferred to a different vehicle.
- Dispatches `POST /gst/compliance/e-way-bill/consignor/bill/vehicle`.
- Updates `vehicle_number`, logs reason (Breakdown / Transshipment), and recalculates validity if required.

### 3. E-Way Bill Cancellation
- Can be cancelled within 24 hours of generation provided the goods have not been verified by a GST officer in transit.
- Dispatches `POST /gst/compliance/e-way-bill/consignor/bill/cancel` and marks record `status='CANCELLED'`.
